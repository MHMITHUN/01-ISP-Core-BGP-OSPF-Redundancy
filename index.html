SQA Case Study

Incident Report: Upstream BGP Flapping & Route Convergence RCA

Structured Defect Report compiled during packet tracer stress testing

Incident ID: INC-2026-NET-014
Severity: P1 - High Availability
Failover SLA: Sub-2 Seconds
Status: Resolved & Verified

1. Symptom & Anomaly Observed

When the physical link to Primary Provider (ISP-A) was administratively shut down (`shutdown` on Gig0/0), edge clients experienced 45 seconds of dropped packets before switching over to ISP-B.

2. Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

BGP default hold-timer is 180 seconds with keepalive of 60 seconds. In the absence of an immediate interface link-down interrupt (e.g. intermediate fiber cut across media converter), the BGP session hung waiting for hold-time expiration before invalidating the route.

3. SQA Resolution & Preventative SOP

Tuned BGP timers to aggressive sub-second values (`neighbor timers 4 12`) and bound BFD (Bidirectional Forwarding Detection) on edge uplinks. Re-testing confirmed failover dropped to 1.8 seconds with only 2 ICMP dropped packets.
